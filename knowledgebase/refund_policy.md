# Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund in the following situations:

- Incorrect order delivered.
- Food quality does not meet restaurant standards.
- Order cancelled before preparation starts.

Refunds are not available if:

- The meal has already been consumed.
- Customer changes their mind after preparation begins.

Refund Process

Refund requests can be submitted:

- In person
- Through the mobile application
- Via customer support email

Processing Time

Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days.

Payment Method

Refunds are returned using the original payment method.
