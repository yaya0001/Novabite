# Event Hosting

Bella Vista offers private event hosting.

Available Events

- Birthday Parties
- Graduation Celebrations
- Corporate Meetings
- Family Gatherings

Capacity

Maximum:
80 guests

Minimum:
15 guests

Booking

Reservations must be made at least 7 days in advance.

Deposit

20% non-refundable deposit is required to confirm the booking.

Cancellation

More than 7 days before:
Full refund of deposit.

Less than 7 days:
Deposit is forfeited.
